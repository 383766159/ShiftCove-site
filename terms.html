3. Estimates, not payroll

All wage figures in ShiftCove are estimates calculated from the hours, rates, breaks and settings you enter. They are not official payroll calculations and are shown before taxes and deductions. Version 1 supports United States dollars and weekly or every-two-weeks pay periods. It does not calculate taxes, deductions, tips, commissions, bonuses, daily overtime, or all state-specific rules. The optional simple overtime estimate requires a supported single-rate, fixed-168-hour workweek setup.

ShiftCove is not an employer timekeeping system, is not affiliated with your employer, and does not determine whether wages are legally owed. Always check your own pay records and, where relevant, seek advice from a qualified professional.
